01 — Numbers first

The annual report, not the press release.

Every candidate starts with the balance sheet, income and cash flow over seven years, checked against the filings. Every flag is explained before a single adjective is written.

02 — Margin of safety

Intrinsic value, against the price.

A conservative estimate of what the business is worth, set against what the market asks. The score leads with it, and the chance of a permanent loss is written down.

03 — Mr. Market
