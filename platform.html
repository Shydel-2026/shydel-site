The value framework

What a business earns, what it owns, and what you pay for it.

Graham and Dodd's security analysis, with Greenwald's two values and Buffett's view of quality. Nothing replaces the margin of safety.

01 — Earning power

What the business earns in a normal year.

Seven years verified, one-offs removed, margins taken across the cycle. Owner earnings, not reported EPS.

Stages 2–5
02 — Two values

Earnings power, and the assets behind it.

Earnings capitalised with no growth, against what the assets would cost to rebuild. A wide gap marks a franchise.

Stages 3, 5 and 13
03 — Growth that earns its keep

Growth counts only above the cost of capital.

New capital must earn more than it costs. Growth comes from the segments and the industry, not consensus.

Stages 9 and 15
04 — Value as a range

A range of outcomes, each with its odds.

Scenarios with their odds, not one number. The decision rests on the conservative end.

Stages 15–16
05 — The margin of safety

The conservative value, against today's price.

40% of every score. The earnings yield must beat the government ten-year, and every thesis ends with a buy-below price.

Stages 16–17
06 — Quality and risk

How far the margin of safety can be trusted.

Returns, cash conversion, debt, the moat and capital allocation decide if cheap is an opportunity or a warning.

The playbooks

The first stage picks the playbook. The framework stays the same.

Its rules then apply at every stage. A company can carry two.

Graham and DoddDeep value

Below earning power, book or NAV. Growth counts for little.

Buffett and GreenwaldQuality franchise

Returns on capital that last. Valued on the return at today's price.

GreenblattSpecial situations

Spin-offs, liquidations, mergers. Valued on the parts or the spread.

Through the cycleCyclicals and turnarounds

A bad year priced as permanent. Valued on mid-cycle margins.

Book valueFinancials

Banks, insurers, holding companies. Valued on book, NAV and return on equity.

FisherGrowth

Fisher's fifteen points, held to the same price discipline.
